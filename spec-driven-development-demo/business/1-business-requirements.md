# Customer Service — Business Requirements

## Product vision
Build a Customer Service platform in which platform users can participate as customers.

The system is developed incrementally using Spec-Driven Development.

## Target capabilities
- Customer Management
- Notifications
- Authentication and Authorization
- Observability
- Deployment and Operations

## Business requirements
- Maintain one unique platform User identity.
- A User lifecycle status is `ACTIVE` or `INACTIVE`.
- Regression testing must protect behavior delivered by earlier increments.
- REST APIs are contract driven.

## Outcome
Create the initial backend foundation and the Customer management.

## Out of scope for this
Notifications, authentication/authorization, messaging, and cloud deployment.
